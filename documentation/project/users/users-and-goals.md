# Odin Stream: users and goals

Status: draft for team review. This document describes intended users and their goals; it does not establish requirements or settle the MVP scope.

## Problem

People watch movies and shows across multiple services and need a convenient way to remember what they have watched, track their progress, and decide what to watch next.

## Intended users

These are overlapping ways people may use the app, not separate account types or fictional personas.

- **Casual viewer:** Find something appealing to watch, see where it is available in their country, and keep a watchlist without relying on memory.
- **Movie or television enthusiast:** Maintain a viewing history, track episodes and seasons, record opinions, and find recommendations that reflect their tastes.
- **Social viewer:** Discover what other people are watching, read and share ratings or reviews, and connect with other viewers. Public profiles, following, and feeds are included in the MVP.

Casual viewers are the primary audience when tradeoffs are needed. The same person may have all of these goals. The proposal identifies casual viewers and movie or television enthusiasts; the social viewer reflects the presentation's social features. These descriptions are working assumptions, not findings from user research.

## Key goals

Users may want to:

1. Discover movies and shows and understand their details before choosing what to watch.
2. Find streaming, rental, or purchase options available in the United States.
3. Save titles for later and maintain an accurate record of what they have watched.
4. Resume a show without forgetting their episode or season progress.
5. Record ratings and reviews and learn from other viewers' opinions.
6. Find relevant recommendations, including when they are new and have little viewing history.
7. Access their saved information across sessions on desktop and mobile browsers.
8. Share ratings and reviews and keep up with other viewers through profiles and feeds.
